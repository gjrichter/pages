Pendulums of change

One pendulum per state, one row per election. Each starts at rest; row by row it swings toward the party that gained — left Democratic, right Republican — by the size of the swing in the margin.

Democratic gain ◀ swings left Republican gain ▶ swings right full swing = 25 pts or more
Presidential years vs the previous presidential election; midterms (House vote) vs the previous presidential. 2026 counts are preliminary (74–99 % counted); DC has no 2026 House vote and stays at rest. Uncontested House seats distort some midterm margins.
